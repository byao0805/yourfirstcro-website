Solutions

Where we create leverage.

Six systems that move the numbers a revenue team is measured on - from first touch through expansion. Each one ties to results you can track.

We've seen these patterns. Here's what we build.

These are the systems that move revenue - faster deals, bigger deals, and accounts that compound after they close. Every one ties to a result you can put on a dashboard.

01 · Pipeline

Pipeline Architecture

What we see
The CRM shows a big number and the forecast is fiction. Early relationship-building and real deals sit in the same stages. Deals stall for 90 days with nobody flagging them.
What we build
Stages with clear exit criteria, a clean split between early activity and engaged deals, and a forecast leadership can trust.
Key Results
Higher stage pass-through rates · Trackable closed-lost signals · Pipeline velocity
02 · Sales

Sales Playbook

What we see
Every rep runs their own discovery. Value gets defended in the pricing call instead of established in the first meeting.
What we build
A repeatable motion - discovery framework, business case, mutual action plan - so qualification happens early and value lands before procurement enters.
Key Results
Faster speed to close · Higher average deal size · Win rate
03 · Account Plans

Account Plans

What we see
Account plans are bloated docs nobody opens, or they're missing entirely. Reps walk in without the signals, stakeholders, or history that grow a deal.
What we build
Account plans that get to the point - surfacing buying signals, mapping stakeholders, and giving reps the next move to close and expand.
Key Results
Higher average deal size · Stakeholder coverage · Expansion win rate
04 · Activation

Activation & Time-to-Value

What we see
The 60 days between closed and activated belong to everyone in theory and to nobody in practice. New revenue stalls before it ever delivers.
What we build
A time-to-value framework with clear owners and milestones, from close to first realized outcome.
Key Results
Shorter time-to-first-value · Onboarding cycle time · Early retention rate
05 · Expansion

Expansion Playbook

What we see
Expansion happens by luck. The accounts with the most room to grow get the same attention as everyone else.
What we build
A system that turns healthy accounts into compounding revenue, with shared signals and a clear play for every expansion moment.
Key Results
Net revenue retention · Expansion ARR · Upsell conversion rate
06 · Operations

GTM Roles & Responsibilities

What we see
The team grew by hiring, not by design. It's unclear who owns pipeline generation, who owns the close, and who owns expansion. Strong performers are stuck doing work below their level.
What we build
An objective look at the GTM org - the right roles mapped to the right responsibilities across the revenue motion, so every seat has a clear mandate and the structure matches the goal.
Key Results
Quota capacity coverage · Rep ramp time · Productivity per head
