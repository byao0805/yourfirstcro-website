About

Built by someone who's been in the seat.

Your First CRO exists because I walked into a revenue leadership role with no playbook, no infrastructure, and no roadmap for what to build first. I'm building the firm I wish had existed then.

Buda Yao
Founder, Your First CRO · VP of Sales, Reflex

I grew up watching my family run small businesses - restaurants, laundromats, dry cleaners. I saw firsthand how hard it is to build something real without the infrastructure to support it. That shaped how I think about revenue: it's about building the system that makes every customer relationship more valuable over time.

I spent 9 years at JustWorks scaling GTM from Series B through pre-IPO - building the playbooks, the team structures, and the customer motion that let the company grow without losing what made it work. Then I joined Reflex as VP of Sales, where I built the revenue infrastructure from the ground up.

Your First CRO is the firm I wish had existed when I walked into that role. The playbook, the frameworks, the customer journey work - all of it came from doing this live, under real pressure, with a real team. I know how to build it because I had to build it myself first.
